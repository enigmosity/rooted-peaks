Land Acknowledgement

We gratefully acknowledge that Rooted Peaks Children's Society operates on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the Métis, Inuit, and our four Nations:
  • Following the Columbia River to the south, the Sinixt.
  • Through the Selkirk Mountains to the east, the Ktunaxa.
  • Following the Columbia River to the north, the Secwepemc/Shuswap.
  • Through the Monashee Mountains to the west, the Syilx/Okanagan.

Nondiscrimination Policy

This Society does not and shall not discriminate on the basis of indigenous identity, race, colour, ancestry, place of origin, political belief, religion, marital status, family status, physical or mental disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, or age, in accordance with the British Columbia Human Rights Code across all programs, operations, and volunteer opportunities.
