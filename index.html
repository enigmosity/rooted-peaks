Dedicated to Making a Tangible Difference

Rooted Peaks Children's Society exists to build healthy communities through meaningful relationships, inclusive practices, lifelong learning, and a shared commitment to the well-being of children

Who We Are

Founded in 2026, Rooted Peaks Children's Society is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to strengthen children, families, educators and communities by fostering meaningful relationships, supporting lifelong learning and creating opportunities for people to grow together. We believe that meaningful change starts with localized action and transparent stewardship.

Land Acknowledgement

We gratefully acknowledge that Rooted Peaks Children's Society operates on the traditional, ancestral, and unceded territory of the Métis, Inuit, and our four Nations:

  • Following the Columbia River to the south, the Sinixt.
  • Through the Selkirk Mountains to the east, the Ktunaxa.
  • Following the Columbia River to the north, the Secwepemc/Shuswap.
  • Through the Monashee Mountains to the west, the Syilx/Okanagan.
